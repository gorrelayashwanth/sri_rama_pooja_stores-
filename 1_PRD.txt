Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Sri Rama Pooja Store — E-Commerce Website & Admin Panel
Version: 1.0Date: March 2026Client: Sri Rama Pooja Store, VijayawadaPrepared For: Development Team
1. Executive Summary
Sri Rama Pooja Store is a well-established physical puja items retailer located in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. The client wants to expand their business online by launching a full-featured e-commerce platform modeled after the ServDharm online store, with their own branding, products, and an Admin Dashboard for complete real-time backend control.

3. Project Goals
Launch a fully functional e-commerce website for Sri Rama Pooja Store
Mirror the UI/UX structure and product categories of ServDharm
Brand the store with Sri Rama Pooja Store identity (name, address, phone)
Provide a real-time Admin Dashboard with full product, order and user control
Support online ordering, cart management, and checkout
Ensure mobile responsiveness and high performance
4. Target Users
User Side: - Devotees and households across India looking for puja items online - Customers from Vijayawada and Andhra Pradesh already familiar with the store - Gift buyers looking for curated puja kits and sets
Admin Side: - Store owner / manager - Inventory team - Order fulfillment staff
